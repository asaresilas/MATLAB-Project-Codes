**Manuscript:** *Multi-Modal Expert Networks for Induction-Motor Fault Diagnosis and Remaining Useful Life Prediction: Real-Benchmark Validation and the Limits of Cross-Dataset Meta-Fusion*
(previously: *Hierarchical Meta-Fusion of Multi-Modal Expert Networks…*)

**Prior decision:** Major Revision · **Revised length:** 9 pages

## Summary of the revision

The panel identified one decisive open question: how the 1,800-sample multi-modal fusion corpus is constructed, and whether the reported fusion gain is real. We investigated this directly against our own pipeline code and cached artefacts.

**The reviewers were right, and the problem is more serious than suspected.** We were able to reproduce the headline figure from the stored artefacts (our recomputation gives F1 = 0.9077 against the reported 0.9089), and then established that this number is an artefact of label-conditioned corpus assembly rather than a measure of multi-modal fusion.

Rather than defend the claim, we have **re-scoped the paper**. The validated per-expert results — in particular the RUL model — are now the headline, and the fusion analysis is reported as a **negative methodological result** with a reusable diagnostic. We believe this makes the paper both honest and more useful to the community.

Three statements in the Round-1 manuscript were factually incorrect and have been corrected (details in §Minor below).

## Response to Major Concerns

### 4.1 — Corpus construction unstated (CRITICAL) · Roadmap P1 #1

**Resolved.** The construction is now stated explicitly in **§IV-C**, with a forward pointer from **§II-E** and **§III-D**.

Reviewer A's inferred reading was correct. Precisely, for each of 1,800 examples:

1. a latent degradation variable `d ~ U(0,1)` is drawn and thresholded into the health state (NORMAL `d < 0.35`; WARNING `0.35 ≤ d < 0.75`; CRITICAL `d ≥ 0.75`);
2. independent Gaussian jitter (σ = 0.08) is added to `d` **per modality**;
3. one **real** window is drawn uniformly from that modality's pool carrying the jittered state.

A fused example is therefore a five-tuple of real windows from five different machines sharing only a health-state label. This is now stated in exactly those terms.

We also disclose that while all **signal windows are real**, the nine auxiliary scalar channels and the fused RUL target are computed analytically from `d` and are **synthetic** — correcting a Round-1 statement (see Minor #1).

### 4.2 — Fusion benefit and disagreement claim (CRITICAL/MAJOR) · Roadmap P1 #2, #3

**Resolved, in the reviewers' favour.**

**Trivial-fusion baselines** (computed, not estimated; `results/publication_metrics/round2_reviewer_response.json`):

| Rule | F1-macro | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|
| Majority vote of five experts' argmax | **0.1831** | 0.308 |
| Mean posterior (= the late-fusion row) | **0.7540** | 0.773 |
| XGBoost meta-learner | 0.9077 | 0.908 |

Majority vote is added to **Table IV**; both are discussed in **§V-B**. We note the late-fusion row *is* the mean-posterior rule and have relabelled it accordingly.

**Why the margin is not evidence of fusion.** §VI-A (retitled *"Why the Apparent Fusion Gain Is Not Established"*) presents three diagnostics on the identical 32-D corpus:

1. **Experts are at chance on the corpus.** Argmax accuracies: CWRU 0.356, Induction 0.392, NASA 0.392, Thermal 0.245 (chance = 0.333); only Current-CNN (0.783) is informative. A genuine fusion of four chance-level experts cannot yield F1 = 0.909.
2. **Each expert block alone recovers the label:** F1 = 0.847 (CWRU), 0.825 (Induction), 0.871 (NASA), 0.859 (Current), 0.882 (Thermal). This is decisive for CWRU, whose three retained class probabilities have magnitude **≤ 1×10⁻⁶** after the 4→3 class truncation. A feature at the numerical noise floor cannot diagnose a bearing fault — it can only encode *which label-selected pool* the window came from.
3. **σ²_p alone attains F1 = 0.834**, although it is generated by the per-modality assembly jitter.

**Disagreement claim re-scoped.** §III-D no longer calls σ²_p "the primary signal discriminating novel fault scenarios." It is now stated as a *hypothesis* valid only when experts observe the same machine, explicitly noted as dominated by assembly jitter on this corpus, and deferred to co-registered acquisition for validation — as Reviewer A-3 requested.

**We now make no multi-modal fusion claim anywhere in the paper.**

### 4.3 — Weak/under-validated experts (MAJOR) · Roadmap P2 #4

## What did not change

The empirical results the panel identified as strong are untouched and now carry the paper:

- **Bi-LSTM-Attn RUL: MAE = 1.354 h, RMSE = 1.734 h, R² = 0.9964** on real NASA IMS run-to-failure data — 28.4% better than SA-PINN (1.89 h) and 57.8% better than CNN-LSTM (3.21 h).
- Per-expert validation on native real benchmarks, including honest disclosure of the Induction-CNN (53.33%) and single-rig thermal limitations.
- IEC 60034-1 hard thermal override; ECE = 0.0567 with MC-Dropout rejected; component-level latency profiling.
